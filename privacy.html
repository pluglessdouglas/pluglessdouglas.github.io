Privacy Policy

RotMon, a game by Plugless Douglas
Effective 12 October 2026

This policy explains what information RotMon ("the game") handles and why. Plugless Douglas ("we") is the developer and publisher of the game.

Playing offline

The game can be played without an account and without going online. Your save file (your character, your RotMon, your progress and your settings) is stored on your device. We do not receive it.

Playing online

Online play is optional. It runs on Epic Online Services, provided by Epic Games, Inc. When you go online, the following is used:

  • An anonymous device login. If you do not sign in with an Epic Games account, Epic Online Services creates an anonymous player ID tied to your device so that other players' devices can connect to yours.
  • Your Epic Games account, if you choose to sign in. We receive your Epic account ID and display name. Your Epic display name is shown only to you. We do not receive your Epic password, email address or payment details.
  • Your game profile. The name you chose in the game, your character's appearance, your team of RotMon and your number of gym wins are sent to the players you meet so they can see you and battle alongside you.
  • A random install ID. A random identifier made on your device is shared with the host of a world you join so that a blocked or removed player stays blocked. It is never displayed.
  • Network information. Connecting to other players necessarily involves your IP address, which is handled by Epic Online Services to set up the connection.

Epic Games processes this information under its own privacy policy: epicgames.com/site/privacypolicy.

What we do not do

  • We do not show advertising in the game.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools in the game.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not collect your contacts, photos, precise location or microphone audio.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Online play does not include free text chat; players communicate with preset emotes. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your save file stays on your device until you delete the game or its data. To sign out of your Epic Games account, use the sign-in button on the game's main menu. To ask for the deletion of online identifiers connected to the game, email us at the address below. You can also manage or delete your Epic Games account directly with Epic.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
