What you do in RotMon

  • Hatch, catch and raise RotMon. Start with your first RotMon and build a team as you travel from town to town.
  • Battle on the beat. Battles are played to the rhythm of the music.
  • Play co-op with friends. Form a party of up to 3. The second player fights beside the leader in double battles; the third is the Hype Man, charging up the team's attacks with a rhythm challenge.
  • Duel and trade. Meet other travellers online, challenge them to a battle or trade RotMon with them.
  • Build a Base Camp together. Grow a garden, cook in the camp kitchen and upgrade your camp with friends.
  • Take a break. Fishing, cooking and other side activities along the way.

Questions

Is RotMon a co-op game?

Yes. You can team up online in a party of up to 3 players. The party walks the world together, the second player fights beside the leader in double battles against wild RotMon, and the third player is the Hype Man, who plays a short rhythm challenge to charge up the team's attacks.

How many players can play RotMon together?

A co-op party holds up to 3 players. Outside a party you can meet other players in shared online worlds, battle them in duels and trade RotMon, and up to 20 players can be members of one Base Camp.

Is RotMon like Pokémon?

RotMon is a creature-collecting RPG, the same kind of game as Pokémon: you catch creatures, raise them, battle and trade. It is its own game with original creatures, battles played to the beat of the music, and co-op parties built in from the start.

Can I play RotMon alone or offline?

Yes. The whole game can be played solo and offline, with no account. Online play is optional.

Do I need an account to play online?

No. Online play runs on Epic Online Services. You can sign in with an Epic Games account or play online without one.

What platforms is RotMon on?

RotMon is being made for iPhone.

When does RotMon come out?

RotMon is in development. This page will be updated with the release date.
