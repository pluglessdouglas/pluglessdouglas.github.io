Privacy Policy

We On Demon Time, a game by Plugless Douglas
Effective 23 October 2026

This policy explains what information We On Demon Time ("the game") handles and why. Plugless Douglas ("we") is the developer and publisher of the game.

Playing offline

The game can be played without an account and without going online. Your save (your character, your bag, your progress, your world and your settings) is stored on your device. We do not receive it.

Playing online

Online play is optional. Playing with others over the internet runs on Epic Online Services, provided by Epic Games, Inc. When you open your world to others or join someone else's, the following is used:

  • An anonymous device login. The game does not ask you to sign in with an account. Epic Online Services creates an anonymous player ID tied to your device so that other players' devices can connect to yours.
  • Your game profile. The name you chose for your character, its appearance, race, element, stats, abilities and pet are sent to the players in the same world so they can see you and play alongside you.
  • What you do in the world. While you play together, your character's position, actions and emotes are shared with the other players in that world. Changes you make in someone else's world are saved by that world's host.
  • A random install ID. A random identifier made on your device is shared with the host of a world you join so that a removed player stays removed. It is never displayed.
  • Network information. Connecting to other players necessarily involves your IP address, which is handled by Epic Online Services to set up the connection.

Epic Games processes this information under its own privacy policy: epicgames.com/site/privacypolicy.

The game can also connect to other players on the same Wi-Fi network directly, without Epic Online Services. Devices on that network can then see each other's local network address.

The game engine

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity Technologies may receive limited technical information, such as your device model and crash reports, under its own privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not show advertising in the game.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools in the game.
  • We do not sell in-game purchases.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not collect your contacts, photos, precise location or microphone audio.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Online play does not include free text chat; players communicate with preset emotes. The name a player gives their character is shown to the other players in the same world. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your save stays on your device until you delete the game or its data. To ask for the deletion of online identifiers connected to the game, email us at the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
