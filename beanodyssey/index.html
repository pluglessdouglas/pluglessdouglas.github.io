Bean Odyssey

Patience over panic. A free action adventure for iPhone: no ads, no in-app purchases, no account needed.

Support

Found a bug, stuck somewhere, or have an idea? Email ashyjoni@gmail.com. It helps to say which iPhone you have and what you were doing, and a screenshot is great.

Controls

  • Drag to walk.
  • Tap to swing, gather or talk.
  • Hold to guard. Touch down just as a yellow hit lands for a perfect deflect.
  • Flick to dodge. Red attacks can't be blocked, so dodge them.

Playing with friends

Online co-op is optional. One phone opens its world from the bag menu and shows a code; a friend types the code to join. There is no chat. The whole game also plays offline.

Where is my save?

On your phone only. Deleting the app deletes your save.
