Privacy Policy

Bean Jump, a game by Plugless Douglas
Effective 1 November 2026

This policy explains what information Bean Jump ("the game") handles and why. Plugless Douglas ("we") is the developer and publisher of the game.

On your phone

The game needs no account and plays offline. Your best score, coins, outfits and settings are stored on your device. We do not receive them.

Motion sensors

The game reads your phone's motion sensors to steer while you play. This is used on the device only and is never stored or sent anywhere.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, your scores are sent to the Bean Jump leaderboard so you can compare them with other players. Game Center is run by Apple under its own privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy. We do not receive your Apple account details.

The game engine

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity Technologies may receive limited technical information, such as your device model and crash reports, under its own privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not show advertising in the game.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools in the game.
  • We do not sell in-game purchases.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not collect your contacts, photos, location or microphone audio.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. There is no chat. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
