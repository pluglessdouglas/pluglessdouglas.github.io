Bean Jump

How high can Eddie go? A free arcade jumping game for iPhone: no ads, no in-app purchases, no account needed.

Support

Found a bug or have an idea? Email ashyjoni@gmail.com. It helps to say which iPhone you have and what happened, and a screenshot is great.

Controls

  • Turn the phone (or tilt it, switch in the pause menu) to steer Eddie left and right. The pause menu can flip the direction too.
  • Tap to throw a red card at a monster. Or stomp it from above.
  • Green platforms bounce, blue ones launch you. Grab the soccer ball, the diamond and the Aura Blaster for a boost.

Coins and outfits

Coins are collected on your runs and spent in the in-game shop on outfits for Eddie. There is nothing to buy with real money.

Leaderboard

Scores go to the Game Center leaderboard (the trophy button) when you're signed in to Game Center on your iPhone.

Where is my progress?

On your phone only. Deleting the app deletes your best score, coins and outfits.
