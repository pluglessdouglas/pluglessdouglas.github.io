RotMon

RotMon is a creature-collecting adventure for iPhone, developed and published by Plugless Douglas. Hatch your first RotMon, travel the world, battle on the beat of the music, and meet other players along the way.

RotMon is in development. Online play is optional and uses Epic Online Services; you can sign in with an Epic Games account or play without one.
