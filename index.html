RotMon

RotMon is a co-op creature-collecting RPG for iPhone, developed and published by Plugless Douglas. Hatch your first RotMon, travel the world, battle on the beat of the music, and team up with friends online: parties of up to 3 players fight wild RotMon together in co-op double battles. You can also duel, trade and build a Base Camp with other players.

RotMon is in development. It can be played solo and offline; online play is optional and uses Epic Online Services, and you can sign in with an Epic Games account or play without one.

Bean Odyssey

Bean Odyssey (formerly We On Demon Time) is a free action adventure for iPhone, developed and published by Plugless Douglas. Play as a DemiGod, Demon, Fairy, Giant or Vampire bean, master patient sword fights, befriend a baby dragon, and restore peace to the Kingdom.

No ads, no in-app purchases, no account needed. Online play with friends is optional and uses Epic Online Services. Support · Privacy Policy
